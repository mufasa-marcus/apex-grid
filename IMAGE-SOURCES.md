# Images and licences

The car image used on the home page is loaded from Unsplash. Its link and the Unsplash Licence are linked in the site footer.

Do not download random Formula 1 driver portraits or team photos from Google Images. They are usually protected by copyright or licensing agreements. For a public portfolio, use properly licensed photo libraries, your own photography, or original AI-generated generic motorsport visuals.

The actual driver names, teams, points, and calendar information are data, not photographs; they are loaded by the public data API.
